## Introduction (3 min)

> Thanks for making time. I'm {name}, and {note-taker} is taking notes. We're trying to
> learn how people {broad activity}, so there are no right or wrong answers, and
> nothing you say will hurt our feelings. We're not testing you. Is it okay if we
> record? It's only used by the team and we'll remove your name from anything we share.

## Warm-up (5 min)

- Tell me about your role. What does a typical week look like?
- Where does {broad activity} fit into that?

## Last occurrence (5 min)

- Think about the last time you {specific behavior}. When was that?
- Where were you? What had just happened?

## Timeline (20–30 min)

- What was the first thing you did?
- What happened next? *(repeat)*
- What tools, people, or documents were involved? Can you show me?
- Where did it get slow, confusing, or annoying?
- How did it end? What did you do with the result?

*Probes: "Tell me more." "What do you mean by {their word}?" "What happened right before
that?" "Can you show me?"*

## Forces (10 min)

- What made you start looking for a better way? *(push)*
- What did you hope would be different? *(pull)*
- What worried you about changing? *(anxiety)*
- What almost kept you doing it the old way? *(habit)*
- What have you tried already? What did it cost in time or money?

## Wrap-up (5 min)

- Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
- Is there someone else who handles this differently that we should talk to?
