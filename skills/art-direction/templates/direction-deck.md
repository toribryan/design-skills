## 7. Crit checklist (1 slide)

Use at every review. Report only what fails.

1. Is there one focal point? Is it the intended one?
2. Does the composition follow the grid, or break it by the stated rule?
3. Does the imagery match the photography, illustration, or 3D rules?
4. Is color within the stated proportion?
5. Does type follow the scale and pairing rules?
6. Would this be mistaken for a competitor's work?
7. Is the product surface using the product column, not the marketing one?

Verdict: ready, or N blocking issues.
