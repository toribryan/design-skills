# Reference Bank

The curated sources these skills point at. This bank follows the structure of
[designeer.xyz](https://designeer.xyz), which is a curated index of interface craft,
component libraries, design systems, AI tools, and design engineers. Its sections are
**Inspiration**, **Visuals**, **Components**, **Utilities**, **Build**, and **Designers**.

Skills cite entries from this bank by name. When a skill says "consult the bank", it
means: pick the two or three entries below that fit the problem, open them, and ground
the work in what you find there, not in memory.

> Maintenance note: entries were compiled from designeer.xyz's public category pages.
> Links move. If one is dead, replace it with the closest living equivalent and keep
> the category.

---
